As a contributor to AyurvedaDaily, you are entitled to the following rights:

1. **Permanent Attribution**:
   - Any approved and merged contribution earns you public recognition in this file and in the Git commit history.
2. **Copyright Over Your Code**:
   - You retain personal copyright over the specific original code and text authored by you.
3. **Open Access**:
   - Because AyurvedaDaily is released under the [MIT License](file:///Users/kishansah/Ayruveda/LICENSE), you are entitled to use, modify, and build upon the software just like any member of the open-source community.
4. **Respect & Inclusion**:
   - All contributors are entitled to fair, constructive feedback during code review, free of harassment or hostility, as defined in our [Contributing Guidelines](file:///Users/kishansah/Ayruveda/CONTRIBUTING.md).
